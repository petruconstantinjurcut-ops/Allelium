Most browsers can translate this page for you — it's built in and works better than any widget we could add:

  • Chrome / Edge / Brave: look for a translate icon (🌐 or "A") at the right end of the address bar, or right-click anywhere on the page and choose "Translate to [your language]."
  • Safari: tap the "Aa" icon in the address bar, then "Translate to [language]."
  • Firefox: install the free built-in Firefox Translations feature, or an add-on like "To Google Translate."

We tried building an automatic translator into the page itself, but the free services this would rely on turned out to be unreliable — your browser's own translator is genuinely the better option.

    Privacy notice

    Accounts are optional. The rest of Allelium works without one, and nothing is sent to the sign-in service unless you open Sign in.

    What an account stores

    • Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google
    • Your quiz scores and the dates you played
    • Traits you bookmark, and any notes you write on them

    What is never stored

    • Pedigrees. They stay in your browser, or inside a share link that you create yourself.
    • Anything you enter in the Inheritance Lab, and what you search for

    Where it lives

    Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and your saved data is kept in Google Cloud Firestore. Firebase also keeps basic sign-in details, such as when you created your account and last signed in. Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in; signing out removes it.

    Who can see it

    Only you can see your quiz history and notes through the site; database rules stop other users from reading them. The person who runs Allelium can technically open the database in the Firebase console, but only to keep the site running. It is not sold, shared or used for ads.

    Deleting it

    Open your account page and choose Delete my account. Your sign-in, quiz history, bookmarks and notes are removed straight away. Google may keep backup copies for a limited time before they are wiped.

    Age

    You need to be 16 or older to make an account, or have a parent or guardian's OK.

    Delete your account?

    This permanently removes your sign-in, quiz history, streak, bookmarks and notes. It can't be undone.
